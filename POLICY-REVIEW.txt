Harinam Joli policy pages — implementation review, 24 October 2026

Completed locally
- Four static, responsive pages sharing the store fonts, brown/cream palette, navigation and footer.
- Footer policy labels are real links on all 15 storefront pages using the shared footer.
- Policy links are available during registration, in the cart, before the payment action, on product detail and on Contact Us.
- Clean policy URLs are supported by Apache and both local servers.
- All storefront pages share the home header dimensions, centered automatic announcements and selected-category styling.
- Search expands beneath the header with a dimmed backdrop; locally hosted Lenis supplies the reference-style scrolling, with reduced-motion support.
- Contact Us uses the shared typography and spacing, with email/WhatsApp contact options and links to order, delivery and returns help.

Flow verified from the frontend, the store API source and read-only WooCommerce settings
- Account required for checkout and order tracking; orders are restricted to their owner.
- Shipping and order totals are validated after the delivery address; payment takes place on a provider page.
- The India payment route is Paytm with INR; cash on delivery is disabled.
- India has an enabled free-shipping method with “No requirement” selected and minimum amount 0.
- Rest of the world has no shipping method. International checkout is also subject to the API's supported-currency checks.
- Returns, cancellations, privacy requests and refunds are support-led; there is no storefront self-service return/refund endpoint.
- Newsletter and contact forms open an email application rather than submit to an automatic mailing/support service.
- Cart/wishlist browser storage, the checkout-attempt session storage (including delivery details), authentication cookies, Google Fonts and Instagram requests are reflected in the privacy text.

Business details still needed for final owner/legal sign-off
- Registered legal entity, the public business address and named grievance officer/designation/contact.
  The configured WooCommerce store-origin address was not assumed to be an approved public registered address.
- The return request window and any approved change-of-mind or product-specific conditions.
- Dispatch and delivery estimates, working days and any destination-specific exclusions.
- Confirm the shipping offer: the banner says ₹599+, while the current WooCommerce India method has no minimum.
  No prices or WooCommerce settings were changed. The policy describes the current free India method without inventing a minimum.
- Refund processing service levels and agreed return-postage arrangements.

Content approach
- No invented delivery/return deadlines, automatic refunds, worldwide delivery promise or 24/7 support promise.
- Statutory consumer remedies are preserved. A successful redirect is not described as proof of payment.
- DPDP implementation is phased; these pages do not assert that every provision is already in force or certify compliance.
- These are drafts based on the observed store flow, for the business owner and qualified legal professional to review before live publication.
