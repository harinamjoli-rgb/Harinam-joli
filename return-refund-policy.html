1. Damaged, Defective, Incorrect or Missing Items

Please report any damaged, defective, incorrect, or missing item within 48 hours of delivery by contacting harinamjoli@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 99130 32130. Include your order number, issue details, and clear photos where applicable.

2. Returns & Replacements

Return or replacement requests will be reviewed based on the issue and applicable consumer rights. Change-of-mind requests are subject to approval and applicable terms. Nothing in this policy limits your statutory consumer rights.

3. Cancellation

Cancellation requests must be submitted as soon as possible. Orders can be cancelled only if they have not been dispatched. Once dispatched, the applicable return policy will apply.

4. Refund Timelines

Once approved, refunds will generally be initiated within 5–7 business days. The amount may take an additional 5–10 business days to reflect in your account, depending on your bank or payment provider. Applicable legal deadlines will be followed.

5. Return Shipping

For damaged, defective, or incorrect items, contact our team for return instructions before shipping the product. Any charges for discretionary returns will be communicated in advance.
