Shopping with Harinam Joli

These terms apply to the Harinam Joli storefront, your store account and purchases made through the website. The Privacy Policy, Shipping & Delivery Policy and Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy explain related parts of the shopping process.

Please read the information that applies to your order before continuing to payment. If anything is unclear, contact us before placing the order.

Accounts and responsible use

An account is required for the storefront checkout and order-tracking services. Provide accurate information, keep your login details private and use an email address you can access for order and password-reset messages.

Only place an order if you can enter into a valid purchase contract under applicable law. If you cannot do so yourself, ask an authorised parent or guardian to handle the purchase.

Do not use the website to submit fraudulent orders, access another person’s account or order information, interfere with the service or publish unlawful or abusive material.

Products, availability and prices

Read the product description, images, availability and any options shown for the item. Colour and appearance can vary with screen settings, but a material difference from the description should be raised with our support team.

Availability, prices and order totals are checked by the store before payment. A product shown in a preview or a “Coming soon” category is not an assurance that it is currently available to purchase. Review the current product page and checkout details.

If a pricing or availability problem affects an order, the team will explain the issue and the available next steps rather than assume your agreement to a different item or additional charge.

Reviewing and placing an order

The checkout takes you through your delivery address, the available shipping/payment route and an order review. You can return to an earlier step to correct details before continuing.

Your order review shows the final total for that checkout. An order reference can be created while payment is still pending. The storefront only treats payment as successful after the store’s payment record confirms it; a page address or a redirect message alone is not proof of payment.

Keep your order reference for support and use the account associated with the purchase to view its status.

Payments and currency

Available payment methods depend on the validated delivery country, supported currency and current store configuration. The current India payment route uses Paytm. Cash on delivery is not available through this storefront.

Any international payment route, including PayPal, is only usable when that destination, shipping method and currency are supported. International shipping is not currently enabled in the online checkout.

You complete payment on the provider’s secure page. That provider’s applicable payment terms also apply. Never send payment credentials, a PIN or an OTP to store support.

Delivery, cancellations and returns

Delivery availability and charges follow the Shipping & Delivery Policy. For a problem with an item, a return or a cancellation request, follow the Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy and contact support with the order details.

Requests are handled by the team; an email or WhatsApp message does not automatically confirm cancellation, a return or a refund. Nothing in these terms excludes remedies for defective, incorrect or otherwise non-conforming goods where applicable consumer law provides them.

Reviews, content and external services

Publish reviews that reflect your own experience and do not include another person’s private information, payment details or abusive content. The name and review content you submit may be displayed publicly.

Links to payment providers, Instagram, WhatsApp and other external services are governed by those services’ own terms and privacy practices.

We work to keep the site available, but maintenance and service interruptions can occur. If an interruption affects an order or payment, contact us so the recorded status can be checked.
