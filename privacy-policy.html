About this policy

This policy explains how Harinam Joli handles personal information when you browse our website, create an account, place an order, leave a review or contact our team. It also covers the store services used for account access, payments and order tracking.

For a question about your information, contact harinamjoli@gmail.com or use our Contact Us page.

Information you share with us

  • Account details: your name, email address, password and any profile information you choose to provide. Account authentication is handled by our WordPress and WooCommerce store services.
  • Delivery details: recipient name, mobile number, street address, optional landmark, city, state, country and postal code.
  • Order records: products, quantities, prices, delivery charges, payment status and order references needed to manage a purchase.
  • Messages and reviews: enquiries, return requests, photos you send to support, and the name, rating and review text you choose to publish. A review’s public name and content can be seen by other visitors.
  • Technical information: your IP address and browser information may be processed for security, abuse prevention and approximate country detection.

Please do not send payment PINs, one-time passwords or full card details through email, WhatsApp or a product review.

Sign in with Google

If you choose Google sign-in, Google shares your account identifier, name, email address and available profile picture with our login service. We use this basic identity information to create or link your customer account, sign you in and maintain your store profile. We do not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts or calendar.

Our WordPress login service stores account-link information and may retain authentication tokens needed for the connection. Your store session is handled using authentication cookies. You can review or revoke the connection in your Google Account connections. Revoking Google access does not automatically delete your store account or order records; contact harinamjoli@gmail.com for an account or privacy request.

How your information is used

We use relevant information to provide account access, validate orders, arrange delivery, show order updates, answer enquiries, handle returns or refunds, and protect the store against fraudulent or abusive activity. Order and transaction records may also be needed for accounting and legal obligations.

Our newsletter form opens your email application. A request reaches us only if you choose to send that email; entering an address on the page alone does not subscribe you. You can ask us to stop promotional updates at any time. Necessary order and support messages are separate from marketing.

Cookies and browser storage

Authentication and store services use cookies to recognise a signed-in session and support checkout. Your cart and wishlist are also saved in your browser so they can remain available when you return.

A checkout attempt, including the delivery information used for that attempt, may be temporarily stored in the current browser tab to help prevent duplicate orders. It is cleared after a verified successful payment or when the browser’s session storage is removed.

You can manage cookies and saved site data in your browser. Clearing them may sign you out or remove saved cart and wishlist items. On a shared device, sign out and clear site data when you have finished.

Service providers and external content

Relevant information is shared with the services needed to operate the store: our hosting and WordPress/WooCommerce services, the payment provider selected at checkout, email services and delivery partners used for your order. We may also disclose information where required by law or to address fraud and security concerns.

Payment information is submitted on the provider’s secure payment page. The store receives the payment status and references needed to process your order. The payment provider’s own privacy terms also apply.

Google Fonts, remotely hosted images and Instagram media may make requests to external services, which can receive technical request information such as your IP address. Following a link to WhatsApp, Instagram or another website takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Keeping and protecting information

We retain account, order and support information for as long as it is needed for the purposes described here, including applicable accounting, legal and dispute-resolution requirements. Information that must be retained for those obligations may not be immediately removable when an account-deletion request is made.

Access controls and authentication measures are used to protect store information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong account password and contact us if you suspect unauthorised access.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can update the profile details available in My Profile. For access, correction, deletion, marketing preferences or other privacy requests, email us from the address associated with your account and explain what you need. We may verify your identity before sharing or changing account information.

Requests are handled in accordance with applicable law, including any limits arising from order records, legal obligations or other people’s rights. If you believe a child has provided information without appropriate authorisation, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

Changes and questions

If our store practices change, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date. The related Terms and Conditions explain how shopping and account access work.

For an unresolved privacy concern, write to harinamjoli@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy concern”. Do not include your password or payment credentials.
